A LITTLE MORE CONTEXT

The details.

What is PROOVD?+

An athlete-led memorabilia marketplace in development. We’re building a way to connect physical collectibles with the athlete’s story and a digital collection record.

Can I buy an item today?+

Not yet. The demo uses sample data and does not process real purchases. Email us to request early-access updates.

What does the digital record show?+

The planned record brings together item details, its athlete connection, and available supporting evidence. The preview here is illustrative; each future listing will explain its own verification details.

I’m an athlete. How can I participate?+

Email cady@getproovd.com with your name, sport, and a little about the items or stories you’d like to bring to fans. We’re welcoming early conversations with athletes and representatives.

How do I request early access?+

Select “Request early access by email” and send your message. This website does not submit a signup form. An email request does not reserve an item or guarantee access.

How is my email used?+

When you email us, we use the information you provide to respond to your inquiry and any updates you request. This website does not create a shopping account or collect payment details. To request removal or ask a privacy question, email cady@getproovd.com.
