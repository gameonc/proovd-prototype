PROOVD is building an athlete-led memorabilia marketplace where every signed piece carries a tokenized chain-of-title record, and every resale on the platform pays the athlete again. Fans tap once to verify authenticity; athletes earn on the primary sale and on every resale through PROOVD. The company is raising $1M in convertible notes from accredited investors ($100K minimum tranche). Milestones are tied to the pilot drop, athlete roster expansion, and first-paid resales.

Platform Opportunity

Direct access to NBA players and All-Star athletes, through a founder who is one of them. PROOVD is led by Cady Lalanne — 2015 NBA draft pick (San Antonio Spurs, 55th overall), BSN champion with Arecibo, captain of the Haiti national basketball team, with pro experience across Spain, Taiwan, the Philippines, and Puerto Rico. His locker-room network pulls in NBA, G-League, WNBA, international pro, and national-team athletes that outside platforms cannot replicate — athletes tap in and join through Cady.

The experience: an athlete supplies a signed jersey with its story. Each piece carries a secure NFC tag tied to a tokenized digital record. The fan taps to verify, keeps the physical piece, and holds the chain of title on their phone. Every resale through PROOVD pays the athlete again.

Memorabilia today depends on one grader’s stamp. AI-generated fakes, forged signatures, and gray-market inventory are breaking that model. PROOVD replaces the stamp with a verifiable record from the athlete.
